bndt by Switchkicker

A warm, mix-ready synth built for Tim "Bandit" Powles. It has Korg- and Moog-style voices, Mellotron-style tape sounds, an Omnichord-style strum mode, a vocoder, and a tape echo, reverb, saturation and shortwave radio built in.

This installs:

  • VST3 for Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig, Reaper and others
  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro, GarageBand and MainStage
  • Standalone app in Applications

You can untick any of them on the Installation Type step (click Customize).
